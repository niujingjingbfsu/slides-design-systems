Art Nouveau❦02 / 07
Principes

Three ornaments

❀

Floral motifs

Lilies, vines, and irises wind through borders and medallions. Nature is the only ornament that never repeats itself.

◩

Flowing curves

The whiplash line — a single, uninterrupted curve that moves like a stem reaching for light. No straight edges where a curve will do.

✦

Muted palette

Moss green, ochre gold, dusty purple on aged cream. Colors that look like they've lived on a wall for a hundred years.
