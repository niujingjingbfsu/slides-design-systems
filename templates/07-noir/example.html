— Principles —

Three truths

I

Black as luxury

Pure black absorbs distraction. Every element that remains earns its place through contrast and craft.

II

Gold as whisper

Champagne gold appears only as hairline rules and small marks — never as fill, never as noise.

III

Serif as voice

Light-weight Cormorant carries elegance. Its italics provide the only ornament the deck allows.
