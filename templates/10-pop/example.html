RULES!

Three big ideas

1

Halftone dots

Red Ben-Day dots in the corners scream comic book. They're texture, not decoration — use them loud.

2

Thick outlines

Every shape gets a 4px black border. White text gets a black stroke. Nothing floats without an edge.

3

Primary power

Yellow, red, blue — straight from the printer. No pastels, no gradients, no apologies.
