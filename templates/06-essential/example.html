ESSENTIAL 02 / 07

Three rules

03 — PRINCIPLES

Design is not what you add.
It is what you leave behind — the quiet confidence of a slide that knows exactly what it wants to say.

  • 01

    Whitespace is content

    Empty space is not wasted space. It gives weight to what remains and guides the eye without decoration.

  • 02

    Typography carries everything

    A single serif family at light weight does the work that colors and shapes do elsewhere.

  • 03

    One accent, used sparingly

    A deep-blue square appears only where attention must land. It never decorates.

ESSENTIAL 03 / 07

Form & restraint

— 01

Serif as structure

Cormorant Garamond at light weight provides elegance and authority without shouting. Its high contrast strokes create visual interest that no decoration could improve upon.

300 weight · 120px display

— 02

Space as voice

Generous margins and sparse composition make every word feel deliberate. The audience does not work to find meaning — the layout presents it.
